# AI assistance in this course

Unless an exercise says otherwise, AI tools may be used after you make an initial prediction or design attempt. You remain responsible for every submitted claim and line of code.

When AI assistance is used in assessed work, record:

1. what you asked;
2. what the system suggested;
3. what you accepted, modified, or rejected;
4. how you tested or reasoned about correctness.

An AI response is not evidence that a result is correct. Suitable evidence includes a derivation, a boundary test, a counterexample, comparison with a trusted reference, or an independently understood test.

You should be able to explain submitted work in your own words and make a small modification during class.
